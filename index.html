About Blue Hour Coffee

in the quiet
turning of light.

Blue Hour Coffee was created for the quiet moments — inspired by the soft light just before sunrise and after sunset. A place to slow down, settle in, and enjoy coffee made with care. Every cup we serve is rooted in quality, warmth, and attention to detail — from thoughtfully sourced beans, to espresso pulled with precision, to ceremonial-grade matcha.

We believe coffee should feel both familiar and special. Whether you're stopping by for your morning latte, meeting a friend, working for the afternoon, or taking a moment for yourself, our space on Milwaukee's East Side is designed to feel welcoming, calm, and comfortable.

At Blue Hour Coffee, we're more than a coffee shop. We're a neighborhood gathering place built around good coffee, genuine hospitality, and the simple beauty of taking your time.

Come in, stay a while, and enjoy the hour.
